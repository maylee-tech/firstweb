Personal Care Packaging Pouch

Liquid-tight · Chemical Resistant · Custom Spout & Zipper

From refillable hand soap to premium shampoo, the personal care industry relies on flexible packaging that is both durable and visually appealing. Our customized personal care pouches are engineered to handle aggressive chemicals, oils, and liquids without leaking or degrading.

We offer a wide range of formats including spout pouches for easy dispensing of liquids, and stand-up zipper pouches for lotions, creams, and solid products. Paired with vibrant print quality, our packaging helps your brand stand out in the retail aisle or e-commerce unboxing.

Specifications

  • Bag styles: Spout pouch, Stand-up zipper pouch, Flat bottom bag, Sachet
  • Material structure: PET/PE, PET/AL/PE, PET/VMPET/PE, or Mono-PE (recyclable options available)
  • Chemical Resistance: High resistance to acids, alkalis, oils, and surfactants
  • Features: Leak-proof spouts (corner or center), high-strength zippers, tear notches, hang holes
  • Capacity: 10ml to 5L (custom sizes available)
  • Printing: Up to 10 colors gravure or digital printing, matte/gloss finish
  • Applications: Shampoo, body wash, liquid soap, laundry detergent, lotion, facial mask, wet wipes
