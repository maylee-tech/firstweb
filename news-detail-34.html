Meet Shenying Packing at IPFM 2026: Showcasing Sustainable & Recyclable Flexible Packaging Solutions

We are thrilled to announce our official participation in the 5th International Plant Fiber Molding Industry Exhibition (IPFM 2026), held from October 28 to October 30, 2026, at the Tanzhou International Convention and Exhibition Center in Foshan, China.

Visit us at Booth A613 to explore the future of eco-friendly, sustainable, and high-performance flexible packaging!

Driving the Green Transition in Packaging

As global environmental regulations tighten and brand owners increasingly prioritize sustainability, the demand for green packaging alternatives has reached an all-time high. At IPFM 2026, Shenying Packing is proud to present our latest lineup of 100% recyclable and biodegradable packaging solutions designed to help global brands reduce their carbon footprint without compromising on protective barrier performance or retail shelf appeal.

What We Are Featuring at Booth A613

  • Mono-Material Recyclable Pouches: Advanced single-material structures (PE/PE or PP/PP) that retain excellent barrier properties while being fully recyclable in standard recycling streams.
  • Biodegradable & Compostable Solutions: Eco-conscious materials engineered for food, pet food, specialty coffee, and consumer goods packaging.
  • High-Performance Barrier Technology: Proving that sustainable packaging can still deliver exceptional protection against moisture, oxygen, and aromas to extend product shelf life.

Connecting with Global Partners

IPFM serves as a vital bridge connecting industry leaders, material innovators, and international buyers. Our dedicated team of packaging experts is on-site at Booth A613 to discuss customized structural designs, eco-transition strategies, and how our flexible manufacturing capabilities can support your brand's green goals.

Whether you are looking to upgrade your current product packaging to fully recyclable formats or searching for a reliable export manufacturing partner, we invite you to stop by, check out our live product samples, and start a conversation with us.

Exhibition Information

  • Exhibition: IPFM 2026 (Foshan, China)
  • Date: October 28–30, 2026
  • Location: Tanzhou International Convention and Exhibition Center
  • Booth: A613
