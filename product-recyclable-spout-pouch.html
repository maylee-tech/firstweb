Recyclable Spout Pouch

Mono-Material · Leak-Proof · High Barrier for Liquids

The spout pouch is the ultimate convenience format for liquid packaging—but traditional multi-material structures are difficult to recycle. Our Recyclable Spout Pouch solves this by utilizing advanced 100% mono-material (Mono-PE or Mono-PP) structures.

Engineered for excellent puncture resistance and hermetic sealing, this pouch is perfect for fruit purees, juices, sauces, liquid detergents, and personal care refills. It delivers the durability your product needs while fully complying with global sustainability standards like the EU PPWR.

Specifications

  • Material structure: Mono-PE (PE/PE) or Mono-PP (PP/PP) with EVOH barrier options
  • Recyclability: Fully compatible with standard PE or PP recycling streams (designed for mechanical recycling)
  • Spout Type: Corner or center placement; standard sizes (e.g., 10mm, 16mm, 22mm) or custom
  • Cap Options: Screw cap, flip-top cap, tamper-evident cap (recyclable mono-material caps available)
  • Capacity: 100ml to 5L (custom sizes available)
  • Printing: Up to 10 colors gravure, matte/gloss finish
  • Applications: Fruit puree, juice, baby food, liquid detergent, shampoo refills, cosmetic gels
