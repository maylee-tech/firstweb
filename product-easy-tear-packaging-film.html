High-Speed Easy-Tear Packaging Film

PET / AL / Bi-directional Easy-Tear PE

For single-serve powders, liquids, and condiments, the packaging experience begins with the tear. A sachet that stretches or tears unevenly frustrates consumers and causes spills. However, making a film easy to tear often conflicts with the mechanical strength required for high-speed packaging machines.

Our High-Speed Easy-Tear Film solves this contradiction. By utilizing a specialized bi-directional easy-tear PE sealant layer combined with a rigid PET outer layer and an Aluminum Foil barrier core, we deliver clean, straight tears for the consumer, and zero breakage for your packaging line.

Specifications

  • Material Structure: PET / AL / Bi-directional Easy-Tear PE (or custom combinations)
  • Tear Performance: Bi-directional linear tear (tears straight in both horizontal and vertical directions without jagged edges)
  • Machine Compatibility: Engineered for high-speed vertical form fill seal (VFFS) and stick pack machines. High tensile strength to prevent web breaks.
  • Barrier Properties: High oxygen and moisture barrier (thanks to the AL layer) to protect sensitive powders and liquids
  • Thickness: Typically 70µm - 110µm (customizable)
  • Printing: Up to 10 colors gravure, ideal for high-resolution branding on small sachets
  • Applications: Coffee stick packs, powdered drink mixes, instant soup, shampoo sachets, ketchup, single-serve condiments
