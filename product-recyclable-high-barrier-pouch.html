Recyclable High-Barrier Pouch

Mono-material · Flat Bottom & Stand-Up Zipper · High Barrier

The demand for sustainable packaging is growing, but protecting your product is still the top priority. Our Recyclable High-Barrier Pouches solve this dilemma by combining 100% mono-material structures (PE/PE or PP/PP) with advanced barrier technology.

Available in both flat bottom (eight-side seal) and stand-up zipper formats, these pouches offer premium shelf presence while remaining fully compatible with standard mechanical recycling streams. Perfect for coffee, dry foods, pet food, and personal care products.

Specifications

  • Bag styles: Eight-side seal flat bottom, Stand-up pouch with zipper
  • Material structure: Mono-PE (PE/PE) or Mono-PP (PP/PP) with EVOH or specialized barrier layers
  • Barrier performance: High oxygen and moisture barrier (OTR < 0.5 cc/m²/day, WVTR < 0.5 g/m²/day) to extend shelf life
  • Recyclability: Designed for store drop-off and mechanical recycling streams (fully compliant with EU PPWR and CEFLEX guidelines)
  • Features: Resealable zipper, tear notch, matte/gloss finish, degassing valve (optional)
  • Printing: Up to 10 colors gravure or digital printing
  • Applications: Specialty coffee, nuts, snacks, dry pet food, powdered detergents
