Tea Packaging Pouch

High-Barrier · Aroma Retention · Light Protection

Tea is highly sensitive to moisture, oxygen, heat, and light. Exposure to any of these elements can cause the delicate essential oils to degrade, resulting in a loss of flavor, aroma, and freshness.

Our tea packaging pouches are engineered with high-barrier materials (such as Aluminum Foil or Metallized PET) to provide exceptional protection against environmental factors. Available in elegant flat bottom and classic stand-up zipper formats, these pouches ensure your tea stays fresh from the first scoop to the last.

Specifications

  • Bag styles: Flat bottom bag, Stand-up zipper pouch, Side gusset bag
  • Material structure: PET/AL/PE (Aluminum Foil), PET/VMPET/PE (Metallized), Kraft paper/Al/PE (Eco-look)
  • Barrier performance: Excellent light, moisture, and oxygen barrier to preserve tea aroma and prevent oxidation
  • Special Features: One-way degassing valve (for Pu-erh and fermented teas), resealable zipper, tear notch
  • Capacity: 50g / 100g / 250g / 500g / 1kg (custom sizes available)
  • Printing: Up to 10 colors gravure, matte/gloss finish, spot UV for premium feel
  • Applications: Loose leaf tea, matcha powder, blooming tea, tea bags, herbal blends
